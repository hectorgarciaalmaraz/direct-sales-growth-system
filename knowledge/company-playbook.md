## Core concepts & terminology (map to your company's words)
- **Personal recruit / initiation:** someone you personally bring in as a new consultant (your
  first line). Usually the main driver of advancement.
- **Personal team / personal group:** your personally recruited consultants and their purchases;
  often the basis for team commissions or bonuses.
- **Unit / group:** the organization led by a director.
- **Unit production:** the unit's wholesale activity, usually measured in points or currency.
- **Active consultant:** a consultant with a qualifying order inside the company's active window.
  `⚠️ VERIFY definition (order size / recency)`
- **Beauty sessions / express appointments:** the selling + booking + team-building activity.
- **Qualification period / letter of intent:** the formal process some companies use to enter
  the director rank. `⚠️ VERIFY whether it applies`
