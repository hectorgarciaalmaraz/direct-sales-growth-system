# Platform Guide — Facebook

> Detailed playbook for FB. Config in `config/platforms.md`. es-MX, faceless-hybrid, manual,
> compliant.

## Formats & priority
- **Reels:** cross-post from IG/TikTok (without other platforms' watermarks). Reach engine.
- **Native video posts:** 30–90 s, slightly more explanatory than IG.
- **Value text/image posts:** tips, before/after *estético*, community questions.
- **Groups:** local beauty/business communities — value first, never spam.

## Audience note
FB skews **older** than TikTok — slightly more context/explanation lands well; good for
buyers with disposable income and for the business opportunity.

## Hooks
- First line of the post is the hook (FB shows text prominently).
- Explanatory curiosity: "Esto es lo que nadie te explica sobre tu rutina de noche…"

## SEO & discovery
- Descriptive, keyworded captions (FB search + feed).
- Participate in/seed relevant **Groups** with genuine value; follow each Group's rules.
- Encourage shares ("comparte con quien le sirva").

## Engagement → funnel
- Comment/DM → **Messenger** → WhatsApp.
- Pin a value comment with the next step.

## Best practices
- Repurpose, don't duplicate identically (adjust hook/length/caption).
- Avoid external-link throttling: lead with value, move links to comments/DM when useful.
- No mass unsolicited messages; respect Group + platform ToS.

## Cadence
- Reels/video: 4–6/week (cross-posted). Group value posts: 1–3/week.
