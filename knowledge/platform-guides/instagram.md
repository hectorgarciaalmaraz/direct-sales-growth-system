# Platform Guide — Instagram

> Detailed playbook for IG. Config (handle, times) lives in `config/platforms.md`. All content
> es-MX, faceless-hybrid, manual publishing, compliant.

## Formats & priority
- **Reels (primary):** discovery engine. 7–21 s, hook in first 1–2 s, burned-in es-MX subtitles.
- **Carousels:** best for **saves** (educational value, "guárdalo"). 6–10 slides, strong cover.
- **Stories:** warm-up, polls/questions, behind-the-scenes (layer C), drive DMs.
- **Lives (occasional):** trust + recruiting (layer C).

## Hooks that work here
- Visual pattern interrupt in frame 1 (textura, gesto, texto grande).
- First caption line = a second hook (curiosity/value).
- Differentiator angle: "Empecé este negocio sin audiencia — esto aprendí." (adapt to `key_differentiator`)

## Hashtags & SEO
- 3–8 **specific** hashtags: niche + local mix (`#skincaremexico #rutinadeskincare #cdmx
  #cuidadodelapiel` + your neighborhood). Avoid generic mega-tags only.
- Keyword the **caption** (first line + naturally throughout) and **alt text**.
- Cover text on Reels/Carousels should contain the keyword.

## Engagement → funnel
- Comment-keyword CTA ("comenta 'RUTINA' y te paso los pasos") → DM → WhatsApp.
- Reply to every early comment; saves & shares > likes for reach.
- Story CTAs: "mándame DM 'INFO'".

## Best practices
- Post Reels consistently (daily if possible); reuse trending audio where it fits.
- Keep a recognizable visual brand (soft-pink, lockups) for recall.
- Cross-post Reels to FB; don't paste IG watermarks on TikTok.
- No engagement pods, no mass DMs (compliance).

## Cadence
- Reels: daily target. Carousels: 2–3/week. Stories: daily light touch.
