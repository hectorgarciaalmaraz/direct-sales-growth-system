# Platform Guide — TikTok

> Detailed playbook for TikTok — the strongest cold-start discovery channel. Config in
> `config/platforms.md`. es-MX, faceless-hybrid, manual, compliant.

## Formats & priority
- **Native short video (primary):** 15–34 s for reach, 34–60 s for depth/SEO.
- **Series / episodic:** named recurring formats ("Mitos de skincare #1, #2…").
- **Reply-with-video:** answer comments as new videos (compounds reach).
- **Trends:** adapt trending formats/sounds to a pillar (don't chase blindly).

## Hooks (critical — first 1 second)
- Say the **keyword out loud** in second 1 + put it on screen.
- Pattern interrupt visual or bold-but-true line.
- The differentiator angle performs as curiosity: "Lo armo en mis ratos libres…" (from `key_differentiator`).

## SEO (TikTok = search engine — high priority)
- Spoken keyword + on-screen text keyword + caption keyword + a few keyworded hashtags.
- Target real searches: "rutina skincare piel grasa", "cómo aplicar sérum", "negocio desde casa
  cdmx".
- Consistent series help TikTok categorize and recommend you.

## Engagement → funnel
- Strong CTA to comment a keyword; reply to comments (text + video).
- comment → DM → WhatsApp (link-in-bio rules vary by account status; check current eligibility).

## Best practices
- **Volume + completion + rewatch** drive reach — keep it tight, loop-friendly endings.
- Burned-in es-MX captions; clean, branded look.
- No reposting with competitor watermarks; original edits perform better.
- No banned automation / engagement schemes (compliance).

## Cadence
- 1–2+ posts/day target during the cold-start ramp; batch-produce.
