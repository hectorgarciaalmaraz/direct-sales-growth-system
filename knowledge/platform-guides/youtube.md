# Platform Guide — YouTube

> Detailed playbook for YouTube — best **evergreen SEO** of the four. Config in
> `config/platforms.md`. es-MX, faceless-hybrid, manual, compliant.

## Formats & priority
- **Shorts (primary for volume/discovery):** ≤ 60 s, vertical, hook in 1 s, es-MX subtitles.
- **Long-form (evergreen SEO):** 5–10 min on searchable topics (build 1/week when capacity
  allows). Durable traffic + authority + recruiting trust.

## Hooks
- Shorts: same 1-second rule as TikTok.
- Long-form: title + thumbnail promise + spoken hook in first 15 s; tease payoff.

## SEO (high priority)
- **Title:** front-load the keyword ("Rutina de skincare para piel grasa | paso a paso").
- **Description:** keyword in first 2 lines; full context; WhatsApp/next-step link.
- **Tags + chapters** (long-form); spoken keyword in intro.
- Shorts also benefit from keyworded title + description.

## Engagement → funnel
- Description link + **pinned comment** with the next step → WhatsApp.
- Long-form is ideal for the honest business explanation (recruiting) — people join people.

## Best practices
- Shorts feed the channel; long-form converts trust. Interlink them (Short → "video completo").
- Consistent branded thumbnails/lower-thirds.
- Reuse pillar topics that match real search demand; update evergreen videos over time.
- Licensed/royalty-free audio only.

## Cadence
- Shorts: daily target. Long-form: ~1/week (evergreen) when bandwidth allows.
