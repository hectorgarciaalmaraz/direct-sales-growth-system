# roadmap-to-top-rank.md — Phases to the top rank (leading indicators, no timelines)

> The company's top leadership rank (`target_rank`) is a **multi-year organization-building** goal. This roadmap has **no
> promised timelines and no income projections**. Each phase advances on **leading indicators**
> we control. Maps to the ladder in `knowledge/company-playbook.md` and the KPIs in
> `config/goals-kpis.md`. `roadmap-strategist` recomputes the current phase from real data.

## How to use
- You are always in exactly one **focus phase** (you can work on the next one's inputs early).
- Advance when the phase's **exit indicators** are consistently met (not a single lucky week).
- Review weekly (`ops/weekly-review.md`); adjust the plan, never the honesty rules.

---

## Phase 1 — First sales & first leads
**Goal:** establish the engine and hit the daily sales floor.
- **Inputs to drive:** posting cadence (TikTok+IG daily), hooks, social SEO, comment→DM→WhatsApp.
- **Leading indicators:** posts/week ↑, reach/saves ↑, DMs opened, new leads/week.
- **Exit when:** consistent ≥1 sale/day for several weeks; 10–20+ leads in CRM; cadence stable.

## Phase 2 — First active team
**Goal:** hit the recruit floor reliably; start a small active team.
- **Inputs:** opportunity content (layer C + differentiator angle), honest invitations, lead magnets,
  business presentations.
- **Leading indicators:** presentations booked/week, initiations/week (≥1), month-2 retention.
- **Exit when:** ≥1 recruit/week sustained; 1–3 active initiates; basic onboarding repeatable.

## Phase 3 — Team leader / future director
**Goal:** grow personal initiations + active team and unit-style activity.
- **Inputs:** scale recruiting + retention; develop initiates into producers; deepen content
  series.
- **Leading indicators:** active team size, personal initiations trend, group purchasing/unit
  activity trend. `⚠️ VERIFY exact rank thresholds in knowledge/company-playbook.md`
- **Exit when:** sustained active team meeting the company's team-leader/future-director criteria.

## Phase 4 — Director qualification → Director
**Goal:** qualify and debut as a director.
- **Inputs:** sustain unit production + active-consultant count for the qualification period;
  complete the company's formal steps (application, orientation).
- **Leading indicators:** unit production per month, # active consultants
  (thresholds from `knowledge/company-playbook.md` — `⚠️ VERIFY current numbers`).
- **Exit when:** qualification completed; debut as director.

## Phase 5 — Develop offspring (first-line) directors
**Goal:** turn strong leaders into their own directors (senior-director progression).
- **Inputs:** leadership development, duplication of the system, mentoring future directors.
- **Leading indicators:** # first-line directors, # of them reaching a senior rank.
- **Exit when:** approaching the top-rank requirements.

## Phase 6 — Top leadership rank
**Goal:** complete the company's qualification process for `target_rank`.
- **Requirement:** a number of first-line directors (some at a senior rank) plus production/tenure
  conditions — `⚠️ VERIFY` the exact current requirements and record them in
  `knowledge/company-playbook.md`.
- **Leading indicators:** first-line director count, senior director count, org production
  trend, retention.

---

## Standing rules across all phases
- **No promises** of income or dates — ever (compliance).
- Optimize **inputs**; let outputs compound.
- Re-evaluate phase + plan in the weekly review; record changes in `ops/changelog.md`.
